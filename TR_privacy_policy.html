Privacy Policy

Talking Ringtones does not acquire any information about you the user: nothing at all.

Talking Ringtones is completely ad free, and it neither acquires nor shares anything at all.

TR requests Record Audio permission so that it can record your new ringtones.

TR requests Write Contacts permission so that it can attach your new ringtones to desired Contacts.

If you want to set a default ringtone, TR must ask permission since that's a system setting.

TR uses the above pemissions solely for the stated purposes, nothing more.
